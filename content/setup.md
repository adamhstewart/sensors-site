## How setup works

AgeWell is designed to be simple for families and respectful for older adults. You receive a ready-to-use kit, place the monitors in key spots, and confirm everything from your mobile phone.

---

## 1. We send you the kit

Your starter kit includes the monitors, labels for where to place them, and a quick setup guide.

- Monitors are small and discreet.
- No cameras and no microphones.
- Built for normal homes, no special wiring required.

---

## 2. Place the monitors in the home

Most families place monitors in the kitchen, bathroom, bedroom, and near the front door. Placement usually takes less than 30 minutes.

The goal is simple: capture daily routine signals without intruding on privacy.

---

## 3. Check status on your mobile phone

As each monitor comes online, you can see it in your mobile view.

- See which monitors are connected.
- Get notified if one goes offline.
- Get clear alerts when the system detects a real issue.

You always know if the setup is healthy or needs attention.

---

## 4. Get remote help from our team

If something is not connecting or a monitor needs adjustment, we can help remotely.

- We guide you step by step.
- We troubleshoot connection issues with you.
- We help fine-tune placement so alerts are reliable.

You are never left to figure it out alone.

---

## What you can expect after setup

Once connected, AgeWell starts learning daily patterns and sends meaningful updates when something changes. You get reassurance without surveillance, and support is available whenever you need it.
