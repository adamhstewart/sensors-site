## Your parent lives alone. You want to know they're okay.

Maybe it's your mom in the house she's lived in for 30 years. Maybe it's your dad who insists he doesn't need help. They value their independence — and you respect that. But you also lie awake sometimes wondering: *Did they eat today? Are they moving around? Is today a normal day?*

AgeWell was designed for exactly this situation.

---

## How it works for a single-person household

**Simple setup.** A small kit of discreet sensors placed in key spots — kitchen, bathroom, bedroom, front door. No cameras. No microphones. Installation takes under 30 minutes.

**Learns their routine.** Over the first few weeks, AgeWell's AI quietly builds an understanding of what a typical day looks like for your parent. When they usually wake up. How often the fridge opens. When they settle in for the evening.

**Alerts that matter.** If something shifts — the fridge hasn't been opened all morning, no movement in the bathroom by noon, the front door hasn't been used in two days — you'll get a gentle, specific message in your family group chat.

## An AI that listens to your family — not just the sensors

AgeWell doesn't just read sensor data. It reads the room.

When someone in your family chat shares context — *"Mom said she's feeling under the weather"* or *"Dad has a doctor's appointment tomorrow"* — the AI takes that into account. It adjusts what's considered normal and what deserves an alert.

## Designed for dignity

Your parent doesn't need to wear anything, press any buttons, or change their routine. The sensors work silently in the background. They'll probably forget they're there — and that's the point.

- No cameras or microphones. Ever.
- No minute-by-minute tracking.
- Only meaningful pattern changes are surfaced.
- Your parent can be informed and involved in the setup, or not — your family decides.

---

## Getting started

1. **Choose a sensor kit** that matches your parent's home layout.
2. **Place the sensors** in a quick, non-invasive setup.
3. **Connect your family group** on WhatsApp, Telegram, or SMS.
4. **Relax.** AgeWell learns the routine and keeps you informed.

The system starts learning from day one. Most families see meaningful insights within the first two weeks.
