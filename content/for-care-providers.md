## A better offering for your residents and their families

If you run an assisted living facility, senior residence, or home care service, you know that family trust is everything. Families choose you because they believe their loved one will be safe, respected, and well cared for.

AgeWell helps you deliver on that promise — with technology that monitors well-being without ever compromising privacy.

---

## Why care providers choose AgeWell

**Differentiate your facility.** Offer families something no camera system or call button can: intelligent, dignified, pattern-based monitoring that respects residents and reassures families.

**Reduce incident response time.** Catch early signs of changes in routine — reduced mobility, skipped meals, altered sleep patterns — before they become emergencies.

**Scale without adding staff burden.** AI-powered insights surface only the changes that matter. Your team focuses on care, not monitoring dashboards.

**Build family trust.** Give families direct, transparent visibility into their loved one's daily well-being — through familiar channels like WhatsApp — without intrusive surveillance.

## An AI that learns from families — not just sensors

What makes AgeWell unique for care providers: the AI doesn't just monitor sensor data. It listens to context shared by families and staff in the chat.

When a family member mentions *"dad had a rough night"* or *"she's on new medication that makes her drowsy"*, the system adjusts its expectations. Fewer false alarms. More relevant insights. Better care coordination between your staff and families.

---

## How it works at scale

**Per-unit deployment.** Sensors are installed in individual apartments or rooms. Each unit gets its own baseline and its own pattern recognition.

**Centralized dashboard.** Your care team gets an overview of all monitored units — flagging rooms where activity patterns have shifted and may need attention.

**Family notifications.** Each resident's family can opt in to receive gentle, pattern-based updates directly in their group chat. They stay informed without needing to call.

**Volume pricing.** We offer facility-level pricing that scales with the number of units. Hardware is priced near cost, and monthly subscriptions are structured for multi-unit operators.
