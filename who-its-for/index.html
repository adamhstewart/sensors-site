Who It's For

NiaVico supports families and care teams navigating the balance between independence and reassurance.

Whether you’re helping a parent live independently, coordinating with siblings, or supporting residents or clients in a professional care setting, NiaVico provides quiet, pattern-based awareness — without cameras or intrusive monitoring.

For Families

Most families don’t want more surveillance. They want better reassurance.

When someone you love lives alone, small questions can grow heavy:

Did they wake up at their usual time? Did they move through their normal routine? Was today a normal day?

NiaVico learns daily patterns and gently surfaces meaningful changes. Not minute-by-minute activity. Not constant alerts. Just thoughtful signals when something shifts.

It protects independence — while easing worry.

When Care Is Shared

When siblings share responsibility, communication can become fragmented.

NiaVico provides shared visibility into meaningful patterns, helping families stay aligned without overwhelming each other with constant updates.

One system. Shared reassurance.

When You’re Supporting More Than One Household

If you’re helping multiple parents or managing care across homes, NiaVico keeps insights simple and centralized — without adding complexity.

Each home maintains its independence. You maintain awareness.

For Senior Living & Home Care Providers

While NiaVico is built for families, we also see meaningful opportunity in professional care environments.

For senior living communities, NiaVico can provide an added layer of pattern-based awareness within private living spaces — without cameras or intrusive surveillance.

For home care agencies, it can offer visibility between visits, helping care teams notice routine changes earlier and prioritize outreach when it matters most.

We are currently preparing for our initial family launch. If you are a senior living operator or home care agency interested in pilot programs or partnership discussions, we would welcome the conversation.

Privacy Across Every Scenario

No matter the setting, our commitments remain the same:

  • No cameras. No microphones. Ever.
  • Pattern-based monitoring — not individual tracking.
  • The family bot only reads messages when it’s @-mentioned.
  • Your conversations stay private.
