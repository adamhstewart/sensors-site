About Us

Three dads, three countries, one shared challenge — supporting aging parents from afar.

Our Story

NiaVico began with a question many adult children quietly ask:

“Are they really okay?”

Our parents value their independence, and we want to respect that. But distance makes reassurance harder. The usual solutions never felt quite right. Daily calls can feel like check-ins. Cameras feel invasive. Medical alert pendants are reactive and often unused. Each option solves one problem while creating another.

We’re three longtime software engineers — and fathers — navigating the same generational shift many families face. Over the years, we’ve built complex systems in data infrastructure, AI, and product design — always with the goal of making technology feel simple and human.

When we looked at the problem in our own families, we realized the answer wasn’t more surveillance — it was better awareness.

So we built what we wished existed.

NiaVico is designed to support independence without intrusion — to quietly understand daily patterns and surface meaningful changes, while preserving dignity and privacy.

We hold NiaVico to the same standards we would expect for our own families.

Our Principles

Privacy is non-negotiable.

No cameras. No microphones. No surveillance. Ever.

Intelligence that supports, not watches.

Our system learns daily patterns and surfaces meaningful changes — not minute-by-minute activity logs.

Built for real families.

We're not chasing features. We're solving a problem we live with ourselves.

Honest pricing.

Hardware priced near cost. A subscription that funds real value. No hidden upsells.

We’re building NiaVico for families like ours — and we take that responsibility personally.
